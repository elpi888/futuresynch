What the agent does

Relationship management that usually
needs a higher plan, without upgrading.

Every day your agent reads the CRM, tells each negotiator who to contact and why, and drafts the personal follow-up in the negotiator's own mailbox. After each call it proposes the field updates. Once a week it proposes a marketing email and reports on it. It works on an entry-level plan such as HubSpot Starter, and on any other CRM whose fields are mapped in the Config.

Ground rules

The agent drafts. People decide.

The first two weeks are a dry run: nothing is written to the CRM, and the changes are proposed in a sheet only. Go-live is a decision for your supervisor.

The agent does
✓ Shows each negotiator who to contact today, and why
✓ Drafts the follow-up in the negotiator's own mailbox
✓ Proposes the task and field updates after each call
✓ Proposes a weekly marketing email and reports on it
✓ Logs every run, including the ones that fail
The agent never
× Sends anything to a client. A person reviews and sends
× Invents a fact. A blank means not known
× Creates or deletes CRM fields, or deletes records
× Contacts anyone who has opted out
× Guesses when two documents disagree. It stops and asks

The consent rules in the Playbook summarise UK GDPR and PECR as generally understood. They are not legal advice. Each agency confirms its own position against its privacy notice and, where needed, with its adviser.
