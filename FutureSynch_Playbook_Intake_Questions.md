# FutureSynch Playbook intake: questions for your AI

Version 1.0 · November 2026

You are helping an estate agency complete the intake for its tailored Property CRM Playbook from FutureSynch. Give this file to your AI assistant (for example Claude), ideally with access to the agency's CRM, website and team notes. Ask it to answer the questions below and to return one markdown file in the format at the end.

## Rules for the AI

1. Answer only from what you can see in the agency's CRM, website and documents, or from what the person tells you. Do not invent anything.
2. If you do not know an answer, write "Not known" and ask the person.
3. Do not include personal data about clients, applicants, tenants or landlords. Staff names and business contact details only.
4. Ask the person to check every answer before they upload the file.
5. Question 12 (marketing updates from FutureSynch) is answered by the person on the website. Do not answer it for them.
6. Keep the headings and numbers exactly as shown below, and return the answers as a single .md file named `intake-answers.md`.

## The questions

1. Your name, role, email and phone.
2. Agency name and website.
3. Areas you cover (postcode districts or towns).
4. Sales, lettings or both.
5. Team: how many people, and who handles buyers, sellers and lettings.
6. Your CRM and plan (for example HubSpot Starter, Reapit, Alto, other).
7. About how many contacts are in the database, and is marketing consent recorded for them? (Answer: Yes, Partly, No or Not sure.)
8. When did you last email your database? (Answer: under 3 months, 3 to 6 months, 6 to 12 months, over 12 months, or never.)
9. How do you name properties and files today? Give one example.
10. Which AI tools do you use, and which connectors are available (email, calendar, Drive, CRM, spreadsheets)?
11. The one thing you most want fixed first.
